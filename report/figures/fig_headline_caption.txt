Regret goes to zero, under the proved bounds, at all three places we use no-regret learning. All axes log-log;
y is regret or Nash gap as a share of the payoff range. Solid lines are medians, bands as stated per panel; dashed
lines are proved bounds.

(A) Inside a turn. Nash gap of RM+'s linearly averaged strategies (alternating updates, nash.rm_plus) on real stage
matrices, divided by the matrix's payoff range; line = median, band = median to 90th percentile; n = 35 matrices.
Bound (Proposition 4, theory/full_game.md:82; checked for nash.rm_plus as coded, theory/CHECK.md:24):
gap <= 2 Delta (sqrt(n)+sqrt(m))/sqrt(T) for an n x m matrix with payoff range Delta (linear averaging; deterministic
full-information play), plotted as 2(sqrt(n)+sqrt(m))/sqrt(T), median over the 35 matrices; it holds on every
matrix (largest measured gap/own bound 0.04). Fitted slope of the median for T >= 10: -1.3; bound slope -0.5.

(B) Across games. Average external regret per turn R_T/T of Hedge over k = 4 experts (top, sampled, safest, safe
exploitation), R_T = max_e sum_t r_{e,t} - sum_t w_t.r_t, against T, the cumulative non-forced turns with feedback.
Rewards are the counterfactual stage-matrix rewards r_{e,t} = clip((q_e.A b_t - min A)/400, 0, 1) along our own
trajectory, so this is regret on the surrogate rewards, not on game outcomes. Carried = anytime Hedge whose weights
persist across games; reset = the same learner restarted at each game, compared with the best expert over the whole
run. Bound (Lemma R2a, anytime FTRL with the shipped indexing w_t ∝ exp(sqrt(8 ln k/(t-1)) L_{t-1})):
R_T/T <= (3/2) sqrt(ln k/(2T)) + sqrt(ln k/8)/T, pathwise for any adaptive opponent; it bounds the carried learner
only. Line = median, band = interquartile range over n streams.
