Caption for fig_headline (report/make_headline_figure.py), as in report/current_report.tex (fig:headline). Numbers printed by
the script; panel B is the real E2 run (2026-11-15).

Regret vanishes under the proved bounds at the four places we use no-regret learning (solid: median; dashed: proved
bound; A-C log-log). (A) Inside a turn: Nash gap of RM+'s averaged strategy, as a share of the payoff range, on 35 real
stage matrices; band: median to 90th percentile; bound Proposition rmplus; fitted median slope -1.3 against the bound's
-0.5. (B) Across games, in the real-Showdown run of Hedge against the MCTS bot (E2; 10 streams, 800 games, 22,858 turns):
average regret per turn R_T/T of Hedge over 4 experts against non-forced turns T with feedback; band: IQR over
streams; curves cut at the shortest stream (T = 2056). Carried: the logged weights, kept across games; reset: the same
learner restarted each game. Bound Lemma R2a (carried learner), R_T/T <= (3/2) sqrt(ln k/(2T)) + sqrt(ln k/8)/T. At
T = 2056 the carried median is 0.0042 (IQR 0.0039-0.0046) against the bound 0.0277, and reset 0.0057; carried stays
under the bound on every stream at every T; fitted slope of the median (T >= 30) -0.15 against the bound's -0.51. The
logged weights match those replayed from the rewards to within 0.0001 on nine streams and 0.0092 on stream 9 (a
resumed run, 2411 of 2448 updates logged). Regret is on the surrogate rewards clip((q_e.Abar b_t - min Abar)/400, 0, 1)
along our own trajectory, not on game outcomes. (C) At a search node: Nash gap, in win probability, of decoupled
self-play on 100 real matrices with rewards (1+tanh(M/160))/2, which by Proposition L4 is the players' average regret;
RM+ (full information) against Exp3 and UCB1 (visit shares, one Bernoulli reward per iteration); band: IQR.
Full-information reference: tuned Hedge sqrt(ln n/(2N)) + sqrt(ln m/(2N)). The bandit bound (Exp3-IX, delta = 0.05)
is a reference, not proved for Exp3 or UCB1. (D) Opponent model: cumulative log-loss regret R_T of the style posterior
against the best fixed style, on the 8 clean streams of the run whose safe-exploit expert uses the style posterior
(BH), against feedback turns T (linear axes); one line per stream, with the median lying on the dashed ln 5 line. The regret equals ln 5 plus
the log of the posterior on the best style, so it meets the bound whenever the posterior settles on that style: it
dips to ~0.9 after the first 50 turns (0.45 at the very start) and returns to ln 5 at the end of every stream (max
excess 5e-4, log rounding).

Methods detail (not in the caption; sources for the numbers above)

(A) Inside a turn. Nash gap of RM+'s linearly averaged strategies (alternating updates, nash.rm_plus) on real stage
matrices, divided by the matrix's payoff range; line = median, band = median to 90th percentile; n = 35 matrices.
Bound (Proposition 4, theory/full_game.md:82; checked for nash.rm_plus as coded, theory/CHECK.md:24):
gap <= 2 Delta (sqrt(n)+sqrt(m))/sqrt(T) for an n x m matrix with payoff range Delta (linear averaging; deterministic
full-information play), plotted as 2(sqrt(n)+sqrt(m))/sqrt(T), median over the 35 matrices; it holds on every
matrix (largest measured gap/own bound 0.04). Fitted slope of the median for T >= 10: -1.3; bound slope -0.5.

(B) Across games, real Showdown (E2: hedge vs stock foul-play, seeds 1-400). Average external regret per turn R_T/T
of Hedge over k = 4 experts (top, sampled, safest, safe exploitation), R_T = max_e sum_t r_{e,t} - sum_t w_t.r_t,
against T, the cumulative non-forced turns with feedback. Rewards are the counterfactual stage-matrix rewards
r_{e,t} = clip((q_e.Abar b_t - min Abar)/400, 0, 1) along our own trajectory (all in [0,1] on every log), so this is
regret on the surrogate rewards, not on game outcomes. Carried = the logged weights of the anytime learner, which
persist across the games of a stream; reset = the same learner restarted at each game, compared with the best expert
over the whole run. Bound (Lemma R2a, anytime FTRL with the shipped indexing w_t ∝ exp(sqrt(8 ln k/(t-1)) L_{t-1})):
R_T/T <= (3/2) sqrt(ln k/(2T)) + sqrt(ln k/8)/T, pathwise for any adaptive opponent; it bounds the carried learner
only. Line = median, band = interquartile range over n = 10 streams (800 games, 22,858 turns; 2,056-2,415 turns per
stream); curves cut at the shortest stream, T = 2056. At T = 2056: carried median 0.0042 (IQR 0.0039-0.0046), reset
0.0057, bound 0.0277. Carried stays under the bound on every stream at every T (largest excess -0.0229). Fitted slope
of the carried median for T >= 30: -0.15; bound -0.51. Log check: the logged weights match the weights replayed from
the logged rewards within 0.0092 (max abs difference). Data: engine/showdown/results/E2/replays_headless/*.A.decisions.jsonl,
via make_headline_figure.py --e2 (streams = sorted seeds[k::10], as batch.split_streams).

(C) At a search node. Nash gap of decoupled self-play at a single node, on real stage matrices with rewards
(1+tanh(M/160))/2 in [0,1], against iterations N. By NashGap(x̄,ȳ) = (Reg1+Reg2)/N this is the players' average
regret (for RM+, with weights t from linear averaging). Full-information RM+: average strategies, exact expected
payoffs. Exp3 (anytime, eta_t = sqrt(ln k/(k t)), no forced exploration) and UCB1 (mean + sqrt(2 ln t/n)): visit
shares, one Bernoulli reward per iteration. Line = median, band = interquartile range; n = 100 matrices sampled
from the 601 two-sided positions of 17 logged games (games capped at 300 turns excluded); same matrices for all
three rules. Gaps below 1e-7 are drawn at 1e-7 (RM+ lower quartile at N = 10,000 is 1.5e-8). Full-information reference: tuned Hedge, sqrt(ln n/(2N)) + sqrt(ln m/(2N)) (two players, regret
sqrt(N ln k/2) each; 0.069 at N = 1000), median over matrices; it is a Hedge bound, used as the full-information
reference for RM+. Bandit bound: Exp3-IX high-probability bound (Neu 2015, delta = 0.05), per player
2 sqrt(2kN ln k) + (sqrt(2kN/ln k)+1) ln(2/delta), summed and divided by N, median over matrices; the median exceeds 1
(vacuous) for N <= 1,000 and is 0.92 and 0.50 at N = 3,000 and 10,000. It is the bound for Exp3-IX, a reference
for our Exp3 and UCB1, not a bound proved for them.
