## Dataset used for the probes (measured)

1,629 real stage positions from 20 logged games (equilibrium-sampled, side one, vs MCTS-2000 on side two; the
`residual/raw` pickles), rebuilt with option lists by deterministic replay (`build_data.py`; `h_s` matched exactly at
all 1,629 turns). Three games stalled to the 300-turn cap and contribute 900 near-identical positions (90% pure
saddle); unless stated, numbers use the **601 two-sided, non-capped decisions**. Matrices are in raw heuristic units,
the same matrix the agent plays; "range units" = fraction of the matrix's payoff range. Row = our agent (maximiser),
column = MCTS-2000, whose actual move `j2` is recorded. Bias: one opponent, one seat, 20 games.

## Rejected or deprioritised, one line each

- Regret-based tree selection as an *improvement*: measured negative at the real iteration budget (F6).
- DCFR for the stage solve: worse than RM+ on one-node games here (F5); its gains are in deep EFGs.
- Solver speed-ups of any kind for play: solvers are < 2% of per-turn time (F5).
- Convex RNR (p-mixture) in place of the ε-constraint: same gain, uncontrolled tail (max loss 0.33 at p = 0.5, F3).
- δ_H gate: dropped in RESULTS (X3), not revisited.
- AdaHedge/fixed share as a *win-rate* lever: X6 already tracks in 0–3 battles; keep only inside rank 4 for the theorem.
- Mean vs max propagation: untestable without the Rust tree; poke-engine already propagates means.

## What would change the ranking

- If rank 1 does not beat sampled (0.44) vs MCTS-2000 in 200 games, the heuristic-unit gain does not transfer and ranks 1–2 drop below 3.
- If the leaf fix (rank 3) moves the win rate vs foul-play 75 ms by ≥ 0.05, the leaf line (ranks 3, 9) moves to the top.
- If a learned predictor (rank 2) exceeds ≈ 75% on foul-play's moves, the exploit line becomes the main story.
